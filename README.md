## Run Down

The first time you load into the game, you will be required to create an account. You **DO NOT** need to use your real email or password if you don't want to. As long as you remember your login details, or save your username and password to your browser, you will be fine.

Once you're logged in, you start with **$100** and one **Basic Generator** that produces **$0.50/s**, meaning you can never completely run out of money.

From here, how you play the game is completely up to you. You can either jump straight into opening cases and trying to win something rare, or take your time and upgrade your generators to increase your income.

**How you play is entirely your choice!**

---

## Strategies
The best strategy to reach end game the fastest, is to be patient at the start. Upgrade your generators as much as you can. Don't gamble during this time. Your only goal is to make as much money per second as you can. 

Alongside upgrading your generators, make sure you are buying upgrades in the upgrades tab. At this stage you will be focusing on upgrades that boost generator speed, not luck, etc...

Your goal now that you are making good money, is maxing out the 🟣 Rigged Rig upgrade (starting at $1000 for the first level). You will also want 🤖 Auto-Broker and 🟣 Plinko Singularity, although they are late game. Once you have all these upgrades, you should see on plinko, on every level, even the worst multiplier will still be making you money. You CANNOT lose money if done correctly.

**Extra tips for this method**

By clicking the max button next to your buy amount on plinko, it auto sets your bet to your current amount of money. This lets you drop all the money you have as fast as you want. Since you also can't lose money, your balance only grows per drop.
