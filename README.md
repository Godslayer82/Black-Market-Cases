# Run Down
Your first time loading on, you are required to make an account. You DO NOT need to use your real email or password if you don't want to. As long as you remember your login, or save your username and password to your browser, then you will be fine.

Now that you are logged in, you start with $100 and one basic generator ($0.5/s), so you can never run out of money.

How you play the game from here is up to you. You can either jump right into opening cases and trying to win something rare, or be patient and upgrade your generators.

How you play is fully your choice!
